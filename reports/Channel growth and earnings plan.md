## 0. Verified after the deep pass (10 Nov 2026, evening; details in research_notes/deep_pass_2026-11-10.md)
- **YouTube Shorts money from 1 Feb 2027 is confirmed from YouTube's own blog:**
  - A channel needs **10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days** to share Shorts ad revenue. Below that it stays in the Partner Program and keeps long-form earnings.
  - New channels need 8,000 watch hours in 365 days, or 20M Shorts views in 90 days.
  - → **Long-form is the main ad income for our new channels.** Shorts drive growth, clipping campaigns and traffic.
- **Long-form:** mid-roll ads only on videos **8 minutes or longer**, so our long videos are 8–30 min.
- **Shorts music** halves a Short's pool allocation (1 track) or cuts it to a third (2 tracks), so our no-music Shorts keep the full share.
- **Tax (confirm with a CA):**
  - US withholding with a W-8BEN (India treaty) is 15% on US-viewer earnings only; without it, 24%. Renew the form every 3 years.
  - Income tax: 44ADA presumptive scheme (tax on 50% of receipts up to ₹50 lakh).
  - GST: AdSense income is an export of services, zero-rated with an LUT; domestic brand deals carry GST.
- **Strikes:** Community Guidelines strikes = a warning, then 3 in 90 days ends the channel. Copyright strikes are counted separately.
- **Instagram:**
  - Hashtags capped at **5**.
  - Reels up to 20 min, but the first 3 min matter.
  - New metrics: **skip rate** (swipes in the first 3 s) and a retention chart.
  - **Trial Reels can be created through the API**: the uploader will A/B-test hooks on non-followers.
- **YouTube API limits:** no API for Community Posts, pinned comments, end screens or the Shorts related-video link. These stay as manual weekly checklist items (built into the approval page).
- **Telegram** pays 50% of ad revenue at 1,000+ subscribers, but via Fragment/Toncoin (crypto), so not for us. Distribution only.
- **Calendar additions:**
  - Super Bowl LXI on Sun 14 Feb 2027.
  - **"Dunesday", 18 Dec 2026:** Avengers: Doomsday and Dune: Part Three open together. The movie channel's biggest week, footage-free decodes only.
- **Benchmarks** (creator data; YouTube publishes none):
  - Shorts viewed-vs-swiped 70–80% = good.
  - Average % viewed 80–90% for Shorts under 35 s; 60–75% for 60 s+.

## 1. Bottom line
- **₹5 lakh a month within 12 months is possible but above average.** Clipping campaigns alone rarely get there.
- **The mix that can:**
  1. High-volume clipping on Content Rewards: about 25 clips a day across 3–5 accounts (in the medium projection).
  2. **2–3 retainer clients**: podcasters and founders paying ₹1–6 lakh a month each for clips. This is the biggest single lever.
  3. Story channels earning from long-form ads, sponsorships and affiliates once monetised [V].

## 3. Payment safety (built)
- **Rule:** every payer is unproven until money lands in the bank.
- **New payers:** cap of ₹2,000, and the first withdrawal is at the minimum, to test the payout route.
- **Trusted payers** (3+ on-time receipts): the larger of ₹25K or 7 days of earnings. No payer may hold more than 40% of what we're owed.
- **Ledger:** tracks credited → settled → sent → bank received, with overdue alerts, exchange-rate spread over 2.5% flagged, and fee mismatches.
- **India paperwork alerts:**
  - FIRA within 15 days of each foreign receipt.
  - FEMA: foreign earnings must reach India within 9 months (alert at 6).
  - GST: warn at ₹15 lakh a year, act at ₹20 lakh. Exports count; zero-rated with an LUT.
- **Scam screening for "brand deal" messages:**
  - fake Google sign-in pages
  - lookalike domains
  - password-protected archives
  - free-mail senders
  - "verify your channel" wording
- **Commands:** `python cli.py payers`, `python cli.py ledger add/report`, `python cli.py safety campaign|offer`.

## 4. India tax and legal (verify each with a CA before filing)
- **GST:** registration is required above ₹20 lakh a year of aggregate turnover, including foreign income [V]. Export of services is zero-rated under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) [K].
- **FEMA (rules from 1 Oct 2026):** foreign service earnings must reach India within 9 months. Keep a FIRA/e-FIRA and the purpose code for each [V].
- **Income tax:** YouTube and campaign income is business income. Keep the ledger and evidence for 24+ months (built: evidence store). Consider presumptive schemes with a CA [K].
- **US withholding on YouTube:** submit AdSense US tax info (W-8BEN with PAN) to claim the India–US treaty rate on US-viewer earnings. Without it, withholding can be up to 24% [K, verify in AdSense].
- **Crypto payouts:** taxed as VDA (30% + 1% TDS) and leave no FIRA, so avoid them [V].
- **Advertising rules (ASCI):** paid content must carry a visible label, for videos of 15 s–2 min on screen for one third of the video. Bio-only disclosure is invalid [V].
- **SEBI:** no investment advice or stock tips; education content uses price data at least 3 months old; registered firms can't pay unregistered finfluencers [V].
- **Content laws:**
  - IT Rules 2026: label AI audio and video.
  - BNS 299 (religious sentiment) for the mythology channel.
  - Personality-rights orders protect many actors and cricketers, including against AI likeness [V].

## 6. Analytics and the weekly learning loop (to build when accounts are connected)
- **Pull for free:** YouTube Analytics API and Instagram Insights via the Graph API, with the owner's sign-in.
- **Metrics per video at 48 h and 7 days:**
  - YouTube Shorts: "viewed vs swiped away", engaged views, average % viewed, loops, subscribers gained.
  - YouTube long-form: click-through rate, average view duration, returning viewers.
  - Instagram: reach, watch time, skip rate, shares/sends, saves.
- **Starting benchmarks [K], to replace with our own medians after 4 weeks:**
  - Shorts viewed-vs-swiped above 60–70%.
  - Short clips: average % viewed above 80%.
  - Long-form click-through 4–10%; average view duration 40–50%.
- **Loop:**
  1. Winners (top 20% by retention and views) go into memory as "what worked".
  2. Topic picking favours winning angles and formats.
  3. Posting slots shift to each channel's best hour.
  4. A format below the channel median for 3 weeks gets changed.
  5. YouTube's Test & Compare (3 thumbnails) and Instagram Trial Reels test hooks.

## 7. How the algorithms decide (summary)
- **Shorts:** each Short is first shown to a small seed audience. It spreads if people watch instead of swiping and rewatch, and if satisfaction is high. Views often stall at 1–2K when the first seconds lose people.
  - From Oct 2026, reach drops for re-uploads "without adding anything of your own" [V].
  - New channels get tested without subscribers, so the first frame and retention decide everything [K/V].
- **Reels:** Mosseri (Sep 2026) named originality, recency, testing with non-followers and bigger AI models. Shares and sends weigh heavily. Reposted or watermarked content is demoted [V].

## 9. SEO, titles and hashtags
- **YouTube:**
  - Title: keyword and hook in the first 40 characters, 30–60 characters long.
  - The first 2 description lines repeat the promise with keywords.
  - 3 hashtags show above the title; more than 60 makes YouTube ignore them all [K].
  - Chapters: at least 3, starting at 0:00, each at least 10 s [K].
  - Credits and sources in the description (built).
- **Hindi channels:** Devanagari curiosity titles for mythology; Hinglish and Devanagari mixed elsewhere. Add both scripts in descriptions for search [V/K].
- **Instagram:** keywords in the caption's first line, at most 5 hashtags, alt text [V].
- **Avoid:** keyword stuffing, and titles that promise more than the clip shows (YouTube's spam policy example: promising a full match for a clip) [V].
