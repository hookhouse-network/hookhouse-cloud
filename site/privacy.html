Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how HookHouse Publisher ("the app"), operated by HookHouse Media, handles data.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by HookHouse Media to manage its own YouTube channels. It does not collect data from members of the public.

What data the app accesses

  • When we sign in with Google, the app receives access to the YouTube channels we own, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, and, for our own business email, the Gmail API.
  • It uses that access to upload our videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and read our own channels' statistics (views, watch time, audience countries).

How the data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on systems controlled by HookHouse Media. Statistics are kept only as long as needed to improve our videos, and data obtained from YouTube about other channels is deleted or refreshed within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services policies.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through Google APIs with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

HookHouse Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google security settings.

Deleting data

Email us and we will delete stored tokens and statistics within 7 days.
