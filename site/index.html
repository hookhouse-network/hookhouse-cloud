HookHouse Media

HookHouse Media is a small independent studio that makes short educational and entertainment videos: stories from history, mysteries, sport and cinema explained, science and nature facts, money tips and gentle learning videos for children.

HookHouse Publisher is our internal tool. It uploads videos that we made to our own YouTube channels, sets their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and reads the statistics of those channels so we can make better videos. It is used only by HookHouse Media; it is not offered to the public.
